## Overview

Besides XR, I make small games in Unity. Four of them are on my [itch.io page](https://alitchee.itch.io/), and you can play all of them in the browser. I was the developer on every one: gameplay code, systems and the Unity setup.

## KebabLala

[Play on itch.io](https://alitchee.itch.io/kebablala) - [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/Alitcher/KebabLala)

A kebab shop simulation that started at Ludum Dare 53 in April 2023, and that I kept building for months after the jam. Customers walk in and order, and you build their plates from shelves of food, sauces and drinks like cola and ayran before their patience runs out.

- Several timed levels with a tutorial, where later customers order more than one thing
- A plate system: drag the plate and scoop in all the ingredients
- An upgrade system for new mixtures, and a save system so progress is kept
- Game data in ScriptableObjects, UI animation with DOTween, and a scrolling background shader

## Grove Dome

[Play on itch.io](https://alitchee.itch.io/grovedome)

Made in 1.5 weeks for the B&W Game Jam 2022 by Grovebit, a team of two, with our own music, code and graphics. The jam theme was "Death is a new beginning": in a mangrove forest, every plant dies at the end of the day, and each morning new plants grow from seeds and from fruit that fell into the soil.

- Drag and drop seeds to plant them, then tap ripe fruit to pick it
- Sell fruit to meet the daily demand before working time runs out
- Dropped fruit costs you health
- Runs in the browser and on Android

## Color Switch Clone

[Play on itch.io](https://alitchee.itch.io/color-switch-clone) - [Source on GitLab](https://gitlab.com/Alitcher/hxrc_codetask_2024/)

A clone of the mobile game "Color Switch", made as a recruitment coding task for HXRC. You control a colored ball, move it through obstacles, collect stars, and switch colors so you only pass through the matching part of each obstacle. It runs in the Unity Editor, as a Windows build and in the browser with WebGL.

## Always: Starry Sky

[Play on itch.io](https://alitchee.itch.io/starrysky)

A casual 2D merge puzzle game I made in 2021, in the style of Merge Mansion, set in a glowing night sky. Released for the browser and Android.

## What I Learned

- Game jams teach scope: a small idea that is finished beats a big one that is not.
- Keeping a jam game alive after the jam (KebabLala) means rebuilding it properly, with real data, saving and upgrades.
- A coding task is a small product: it has to be clean, playable and easy for someone else to run.
