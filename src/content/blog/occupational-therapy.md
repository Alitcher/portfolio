# Occupational Therapy

Occupational Therapy is one of the many XR scenarios in VLL, our Virtual
Learning Platform. The project itself is confidential, so I can only share this video
and the parts I worked on.

https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Ve9JcGp30FHR7n4gO0Umoxp1kqeLcOp7/view?usp=sharing

## What I worked on

- Full-stack features across the NestJS backend, the student and teacher
  client applications, and our shared in-house SDK.
- Classroom management systems and the APIs behind the interactions.
- Multiplayer with Photon Fusion, so a class can join the same live session.
- NPC characters driven by finite state machines and connected to Convai, so
  their dialogue follows the state they are in and what is happening around
  them.

## Tech

Unity, C#, NestJS, TypeScript, Photon Fusion and Convai.
